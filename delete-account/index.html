Account & Data Deletion — Viavu

App: Viavu • Contact: hello@viavu.app

You can permanently delete your Viavu account and all associated personal data at any time using either of the methods below.

Option 1: Delete Directly in the Viavu App (Instant)

  1. Open the Viavu app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to your Profile / Settings screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your request.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email from your registered email address to hello@viavu.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request — Viavu".

What Data Is Deleted

Once your request is processed (immediately for in-app deletion, or within 7 days for email requests), the following data is permanently erased:

  • Your account credentials and authentication record (Email / Google Sign-In link).
  • Your profile details (display name, bio, age, preferences) and uploaded profile photos.
  • Your sent and received arrows, matches, and chat messages.
  • Your proximity tokens and push notification identifiers.

Data Retention

No personal profile data is retained after deletion, except minimal anonymized security or abuse-prevention logs if required by law or to enforce platform safety.
