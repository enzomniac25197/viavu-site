Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 • App: Viavu

Viavu ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Viavu mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication details when you sign up using Email or Google Sign-In.
  • Profile Information: Display name, age/date of birth, gender, bio, and profile photos you choose to upload.
  • Location Data: Approximate or precise location data (with your permission) to enable proximity-based discovery and nearby connections.
  • User Content: Messages, interactions, and reports you send within the app.
  • Device & Technical Data: Basic device information, crash logs, and notification tokens needed to keep the app functioning reliably and securely.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your Viavu account.
  • Show nearby profiles and enable in-app interactions and messaging.
  • Maintain safety, prevent abuse, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect user photos and private data.
  • Respond to support requests and send essential service notifications.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your data is stored securely using cloud infrastructure providers (such as Supabase and Google/Firebase services) strictly to operate the app, authenticate users, and deliver notifications. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Age Restriction & Child Safety

Viavu is strictly for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any suspected violations can be reported immediately to hello@viavu.app and will result in immediate account termination and reporting to relevant authorities.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App: Open Settings → Delete Account inside the Viavu app.
  • By Email: Send a request from your registered email address to hello@viavu.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Upon verification, your profile, photos, messages, and account records are permanently deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for safety/fraud prevention.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at hello@viavu.app.
