ViaVu Child Safety Standards (CSAE Policy)

App Name: ViaVu (app.viavu.mobile) · Effective Date: November 27, 2026

Zero-Tolerance Commitment: ViaVu strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any form. ViaVu is strictly restricted to adults aged 18 and older.

1. Strict 18+ Age Policy

ViaVu is a real-world proximity messaging platform built exclusively for adults (18+). Minors are prohibited from creating accounts or using ViaVu. We enforce an in-app date-of-birth age gate prior to accessing proximity discovery or messaging, and we immediately suspend any account suspected or confirmed to belong to an individual under 18.

2. Prohibited Conduct (CSAE & CSAM)

  • Creating, sharing, soliciting, or distributing Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in text, image, audio, or any other format.
  • Grooming, sexualization, sextortion, trafficking, or any exploitation of minors.
  • Using ViaVu's proximity discovery or messaging features to target, harass, or endanger minors.

3. In-App Reporting & User Blocking

Every ViaVu user has immediate, in-app safety controls:

  • Report Users & Content: Users can report any incoming arrow, message, or profile directly inside the app, including a dedicated Underage user or Child Safety concern (CSAE) category.
  • General Safety Feedback: Users can submit safety concerns at any time from the Profile / Settings screen inside ViaVu.
  • Instant Blocking: Blocking a user immediately terminates active chat access and hides both users from each other's nearby discovery radar.

4. Enforcement & Reporting to Authorities

All CSAE, CSAM, and underage reports are prioritized for urgent review. Upon obtaining actual knowledge of CSAM or child exploitation, ViaVu will immediately remove the offending content, permanently terminate the responsible account(s), preserve relevant evidence as required by law, and report the incident to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or applicable regional law enforcement authorities in full compliance with child safety laws.
